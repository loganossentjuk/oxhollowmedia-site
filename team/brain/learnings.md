# Learnings: rules we follow now

Format: **Rule.** Confidence (low/med/high). Evidence. Date.

- **Text on a Reel goes on a solid cream band in the top third; never thin serif over a busy photo.** High. Logan rejected the first TikTok as unreadable (2026-10-28); the cream-band version was accepted. 2026-10-28.

- **A slideshow of finished prints with facts is not enough for the personal account.** High. Logan rejected the Galápagos "lizard sneezes salt" Reel: "show off the work I've done… striving Nat Geo photographer… learning and growing." 2026-11-02.
- **Pinterest pins in the framed-black-on-cream style are approved as a standing format.** High. Logan: "Those look amazing." 2026-10-28.
- **Keep the brand account clean; put the journey on the personal account.** High. Logan's decision, 2026-11-02.
