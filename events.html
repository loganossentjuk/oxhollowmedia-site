For event planners

Questions planners ask.

What do you need from me to quote?
The date, the venue, and what you want to walk away with. The contact form takes about two minutes. Within one business day you'll have availability, a quote, and a simple shot plan.
How many hours does each rate cover?
The Session is up to 4 hours on site, from $1,000. The Day is up to 8 hours, from $2,000. Two-plus-day conferences start at $5,000 and are scoped to your run-of-show.
What do I get after the event?
A color-graded, full-resolution gallery within 5 business days, in high resolution and web sizes. On full-day coverage there is also a same-week highlight set for social.
Do you photograph nonprofit galas and fundraisers?
Yes. Galas, benefit dinners, and conservation and community events are a big part of my work.
Do you bring a second shooter?
Yes. Add one to any booking for half the package rate: $500 on The Session, $1,000 on The Day. A second shooter is already part of The Production.
Can you shoot photo and video at the same event?
Yes. The Production covers an event plus a brand film together. I also make short recap films from events. Film projects start at $6,500.
What if we need content every month?
The monthly content partner plan starts at $2,500 a month. It is one shoot day a month, covering events, culture, or recruiting. It also includes 8–10 social cut-downs and priority booking.
Where do you work, and is travel included?
San Francisco, Oakland, San Jose, the Peninsula, and the rest of the Bay Area. Rates are starting points for Bay Area bookings. Outside the Bay Area, a travel day is $500, plus the cost of travel.
How many photos will we get?
Roughly 100 edited photos per hour of coverage. You get my selected edits, not every frame. RAW files are available if you ask.
How can we use the photos?
However you like. Once the gallery is delivered, the photos are yours to use across your website, social, press, and internal channels.
What if the event runs long?
I stay. Extra time is $250 an hour.
Can you take a last-minute booking?
Often, yes. Rush jobs are a $200 add-on. If you can, book at least 24 hours ahead.
Will you sign an NDA?
Yes. If your event is confidential, send it over with the booking.
What is it like having you in the room?
Candid, unobtrusive, editorial. I work the room without interrupting it.

Something I haven't covered? Ask me directly. You'll hear back within one business day.
