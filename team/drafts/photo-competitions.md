## Flags: rules that could exclude his images

1. **Watermarks.** Every file in `images/portfolio/` carries the Ox Hollow mark, and contests reject watermarks and borders (BCDC and Audubon say so explicitly). **Export entries from the original masters, never from the site files.** The same goes for Shark Shadow's bordered A4 print file: submit the unbordered image.
2. **Salt and Stone editing.**
   - WPY Natural Artistry allows in-camera multiple exposures only, and nothing can be added or removed.
   - BigPicture Art of Nature says effects must not come from digital manipulation.
   - **TODO(Logan):** are Obsidian Palace, Phase Shift and Divine Duality single frames with only global edits (tone, contrast, crop)? If yes, they are eligible everywhere. If they involve blending or heavy local work, send them only to the **Sony Creative** category.
3. **RAW proof.** WPY requires RAWs or original files from the shortlist. Audubon and Nature's Best may ask. **TODO(Logan):** do the 2019 Galápagos RAWs still exist?
4. **Captive or cultivated subjects.** No captive animals are known in his work. The petunias, geraniums and hydrangea are cultivated garden plants: right for IGPOTY, wrong for WPY's wild-subject rule.
5. **People.** Paddler at the Gate and any Ocean Defenders stills show people. Use only frames where the people aren't recognisable, or ones Logan has cleared (this matches the team's existing rule).
6. **Film eligibility.** Ocean Defenders (2023) and Fluid Frontier (2018) are probably too old for Jackson Wild and Wild & Scenic, and both are public on Vimeo. IOFF is the only plausible near-term festival; check its rules first.
7. **AI.** WPY, BCDC and the Image Flow ban AI-generated or AI-added content. Avoid generative fill and AI noise tools that add detail, or read each contest's AI page.
8. **Rights.** Some contests take broad usage licences. One photographer's blog warned about a past Sierra Club contest that took a perpetual, cost-free licence [36]. Read the licence clause before entering any free contest.

He photographs ocean, mountains and wildlife, from the Eastern Sierra and the Pacific Northwest to the Galápagos Islands. ### Artist statement, 100 words (first person; leads with Salt and Stone)

> I've been making pictures since 2017, mostly where land meets water. I want to bring people closer to nature, and to tell stories of conservation, cultivation and care.
>
> Salt and Stone began at the Mermaid's Pools on Oahu. I shot from inside a volcanic cave as the waves crashed in. Black rock, white water, a little light. Up close, the shoreline stops being a place and becomes a shape.
>
> The same patience runs through my other work: blue hour over the Eastern Sierra, and a humpback breaching in the Galápagos.
>
> My studio's tagline is two words long: dare to see.
