Privacy

Privacy.

A short, plain note on what this site measures and who handles your details.

What I measure
I use Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages, posts and ads bring people to the site, and which pages lead to an enquiry or a print order. I've turned off Google's ad personalisation and Google signals, so visits here aren't used to target ads at you.
What I never send to Google
Your name, email address or message. When you send the contact form, Google Analytics only hears that a form was sent, along with the dropdown answers (what you need, rough budget, and how you heard about me).
The contact form
The form is handled by Formspree, which emails your message to me so I can reply.
Print orders
Checkout is handled by Shopify, so your payment and shipping details go to Shopify, not to this site.
Opting out
You can stop Google Analytics from counting your visits on any site with Google's opt-out browser add-on.
