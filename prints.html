How it works

Made to order.

Nothing sits in a warehouse. Every print is produced when you order it, on archival matte paper rated for a century of colour. Smaller sizes ship flat; larger sizes ship rolled in a tube, ready for framing.

Tell me the title and the size you want and I'll come back with a quote including shipping — usually within one business day.

Start an order · hello@oxhollowmedia.com

Questions

Before you order.

What sizes are available?
Four, from a desk-sized print up to a statement piece. Which sizes suit a given frame depends on its aspect ratio, so I'll confirm the options for the image you choose.
Can I order a photograph that isn't listed here?
Yes. Almost anything in the gallery can be printed, including the botanical and event work. Send me the title.
Do you sell limited editions?
Signed, numbered editions are available on select frames. Ask and I'll tell you what's still open.
Licensing instead of a print?
High-resolution files can be licensed for personal or commercial use. Get in touch with what you have in mind.
