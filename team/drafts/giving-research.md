# Giving a share of revenue: research (2026-10-21)

Draft for Logan. Nothing has been sent or published. **This is not legal or tax advice. Confirm with an accountant or lawyer before you announce anything.**

## 1. Three structures

| | (a) Place-based, one charity per collection | (b) One main partner | (c) 1% for the Planet |
|---|---|---|---|
| How it works | Galápagos prints fund a Galápagos charity, Oahu prints fund an Oahu one, and so on | All giving goes to one charity, reviewed each year | Join as a member, give at least 1% of revenue to approved partners, use their logo |
| Buyer sees | The strongest story: "this print helps this place" | Simple and easy to trust; one name to recognise | A known badge, but "1%" reads small next to a $155 print |
| Admin | **High.** In California each charity named in a sales promotion needs its own signed contract, transfers every 90 days and an accounting (§3). Five places means five of each. | **Low.** One contract, one quarterly transfer | **Medium.** Yearly certification within 120 days of year-end, with proof of revenue and proof of gifts. You still need the California contract if you advertise it per sale. |
| Main risk | Small per-charity amounts. Some places (Scotland) have no clear partner. | Less tied to each print's story | Costs more than the gift right now (see below) |

**1% for the Planet terms** (onepercentfortheplanet.org, read 2026-10-21):
- **1% of what:** "actual earned revenue/turnover", less returns, discounts and pass-through costs. For most US businesses that's Schedule C line 3.
- **Dues:** $500 a year at $0–500k revenue, plus a one-time activation fee of $200 ($100 if first-year revenue is projected at $50k or less). Dues count toward the 1%.
- **Partners:** only gifts to in-network partners count, and the partner has to accept them. The network is referral and invitation only.
- **What counts:** money, in-kind work at fair market value (acknowledged by the partner) and employee volunteer time can each make up 100% of the commitment.
- **Shortfall:** membership is deactivated below 95% of the target.
- **Logo:** a full-company member may use the logo on every channel and every product. The CERTIFIED logo needs a successful certification.

At the base-case revenue of $48.2k, 1% is $482. That is less than the $500 dues, so in year one the whole commitment would go to 1% for the Planet itself rather than to a conservation group. **Not worth it until revenue is around $100k or more.**

## 3. Legal and compliance (California)

- **Commercial coventurer:** a for-profit business that tells the public a purchase will benefit a charity. A line like "a portion of sales goes to X" makes Ox Hollow one (Gov. Code §12599.2(a), [text](https://california.public.law/codes/government_code_section_12599.2), read 2026-10-21).
- **No registration needed if all three hold** (§12599.2(b)):
  1. **Before** any claim goes public, there is a written contract with the charity, **signed by two of its officers**.
  2. Everything raised is transferred **within 90 days** of the first claim, then at the end of every 90-day period after that.
  3. Each transfer comes with a **written accounting** detailed enough for the charity to check the claims and report them.
- **Otherwise:** register with the AG's Registry before you advertise (form CT-5CF, due Jan 15, $500/yr per the AG; the statute text says $200). File an annual report (CT-6CF, due Jan 30). A late fee of $25 a month can apply. See the [AG Commercial Coventurers page](https://oag.ca.gov/charities/pf/cc), read 2026-10-21.
- **Disclosure standard:** BBB Wise Giving Alliance Standard 19 says the promotion should state the actual or expected amount per purchase, how long it runs, and any cap or minimum ([standard text](https://www.nokidhungry.org/sites/default/files/2021-05/BBB%20Guidelines.pdf); [give.org](https://give.org/news/from-books-to-borscht-retail-sales-helping-ukraine)). The FTC has no specific cause-marketing rule, but its general ban on deceptive claims applies: what you say must be true and match what you pay.
- **Safe wording:** "**$10 from every framed print and $5 from every paper print goes to [Org]. Paid quarterly, no cap, through Dec 31 2027.**"
  - Avoid "a portion of proceeds", "profits" and "X% of profits". They are vague and hard to check.
- **Tax note (general only):** a single-member LLC is a pass-through.
  - Charitable gifts generally flow to Logan's personal return and count only if he itemizes (IRS Pub 526).
  - Payments that are really advertising may sometimes be deductible as a business expense instead.
  - Ask the accountant which applies, and keep every charity receipt.

## 4. Economics (12-month cases from revenue-plan.md)

- **Margin** means print contribution (40 × $88 in base) plus event, retainer and film revenue. Service costs aren't tracked, so this overstates margin.
- **Base case:** revenue ≈ $48.2k, margin ≈ $45.5k.

| Option | Conservative ($14.6k) | **Base ($48.2k)** | Stretch ($115.2k) | Base as % of margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. $5 per paper print, $10 per framed print, 5% of events, retainer and films | ≈ $800 | **≈ $2,500** | ≈ $5,900 | 5.5% |
| B. 5% of revenue | $730 | **$2,410** | $5,760 | 5.3% |
| C. 10% of revenue | $1,460 | **$4,820** | $11,520 | 10.6% |
| (1% for the Planet) | $146, below the $500 dues | $482, below the dues | $1,152 | 1.1% |

- **Per print:** $10 is 11% of the $88 contribution on a framed 12x18, and 15% of the $65 on a framed 8x12.
- **Paper:** $5 is 12–25% of the $20–40 paper contribution, so it hurts there. Option: $3 on paper.
- **Option A** gives about the same as B but tells a clearer per-print story buyers can check.

## 5. Recommendation

- **Structure:** (b), one main partner for the first year. Revisit place-based giving (a) once print sales justify more contracts.
- **Partner:** **The Marine Mammal Center.**
  - Local, rated by an independent watchdog, and it fits the ocean and wildlife prints.
  - Logan can give in-kind photo work there and film it.
  - Alternatives: Galápagos Conservancy, for the strongest print tie; Ocean Defenders Alliance, for the existing relationship.
- **Amount:** Option A. $10 per framed print and $5 per paper print, plus 5% of events, retainer and film fees. About $2.5k in the base case. Transfer quarterly, which also meets the 90-day rule.
- **Announce it:**
  - a short "Giving" note on /about and on each print page, with the exact wording from §3 and only after the contract is signed
  - a quarterly post (Instagram plus the newsletter): what was given, a screenshot of the receipt, and one photo from the partner
  - actual numbers only, never projections

**Logan's 3 steps**
1. Ask the accountant two things: is the gift deductible, and does Option A fit the budget? (Optionally, ask a lawyer to read the contract.)
2. Email the partner's corporate-giving contact for a written cause-marketing agreement signed by two officers. Ask about logo use too.
3. Once it's signed, tell the team. We then add the Giving note (copy approval first) and set a reminder for the quarterly transfer, its accounting and the report post.
