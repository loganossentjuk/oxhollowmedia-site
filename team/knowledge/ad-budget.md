# Is paid advertising worth it yet?

Knowledge base. Researched by the marketing lead; sources were read on **2026-10-25**.
Draft only: no ad accounts were opened, and no money was spent.
Logan's standing rule (BUSINESS.md, REVENUE-ENGINE.md): the ad budget is **$0**, except a small holiday print test that Logan is open to, with the amount for Logan to set.

**Citation tags:** [P] = primary, [S] = secondary, **UNVERIFIED** = weak or conflicting sources. Almost all CPM and CPC benchmarks are vendor or agency estimates, not platform data. Treat them as ranges for planning, not promises.

## Short answer

**Not yet for growth. Maybe a capped holiday test for prints.**
1. There has been **no print order yet** (BUSINESS.md 2026-10-13; check the latest Shopify read before deciding). Until the shop converts organic visitors, paid traffic would only buy more visits to a page we haven't proven sells.
2. At typical cold-traffic conversion rates, **Meta's cost per sale lands at or above the margin on a framed print** (maths below).
3. **Etsy Offsite Ads already gives free paid reach**: Logan pays 15% only when a sale happens. Keep it on.
4. **Event leads shouldn't be bought.** Outreach (10 a week) and referrals are cheaper per booked job.

## 1. What a sale is worth (the ceiling on ad spend)

From BUSINESS.md (site "keep" after Gelato, shipping and card fee) and `team/drafts/etsy-launch.md` (Etsy net):

| Product | Site price | Site keep | Etsy price | Etsy net (organic) | Etsy net if Offsite-Ads-attributed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Framed print 8x12 | $115 | $67 | $129 | $70.30 | $50.95 |
| Framed print 12x18 | $155 | $89 | $175 | $95.93 | $69.68 |
| Framed canvas 12x18 | $150 | $85 | $169 | $92.50 | $67.15 |
| Paper 8x12 | $35 | $19 | $39 | $20.84 | $14.99 |

**The rule used below:** a paid sale should cost **no more than half the keep**, so each sale still earns money and pays something toward Shopify's $39/month. For the lead product (framed 12x18), that's a **target cost per sale (CPA) of about $45**, with a hard ceiling of about $89 (break-even). Paper can't carry paid traffic at all, since its keep is $19.

**The Meta maths, made concrete:**
- $1.11 per click ÷ 1.32% conversion ≈ **$84 per sale**. At 1.52% conversion it's about $73.
- That's about the full keep on a framed 12x18 ($89) and **more** than the keep on a framed 8x12 ($67).
- A new shop with no reviews will probably convert below the industry median, so cold Meta traffic to print pages loses money until the page is proven.

**The Pinterest maths:** $0.50–0.80 per click ÷ 1.32% ≈ **$38–61 per sale**. That's close to the $45 target, which is why Pinterest is the stage-2 channel. These are agency estimates, though.

## 3. Staged plan

### Stage 0: $0 (now until the triggers below)
- Organic Instagram, Pinterest and Etsy (see `pinterest-tiktok.md`).
- Etsy **Offsite Ads on**. **Share & Save** links when posting Etsy listings (4% back on fees).
- Measurement first. Every channel link carries UTMs, and the contact form already has "How did you hear about me?".
  - TODO(Logan): ads that optimise for purchases need a pixel/tag (Meta pixel or Pinterest tag) on the site and on Shopify checkout. That's a site change and needs Logan's OK. Without it, judge tests on landing-page views and add-to-carts in Shopify only.

**Triggers to leave Stage 0** (any two):
1. At least **1 organic print sale** on the site or Etsy (proves the page and checkout convert).
2. **A Reel reaches 3× the account's median reach organically.** That's the creative worth paying behind. Obsidian Palace is the current candidate.
3. Etsy listings show **favourites without sales** after about 300 views (interest is there; a nudge may help).

### Stage 1: holiday micro-test (suggested; Logan sets the amount)
Run it **before Gelato's holiday cut-off** (dates still TODO, per `REVENUE-ENGINE.md`), ideally mid-November to early December.

| Test | Budget | What to measure | Kill rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Etsy Ads** on the 5 listings with the most favourites (framed only) | **$3/day × 14 days = $42 max** | Clicks, orders, ad spend per order (Etsy Stats) | Stop if **$25 is spent with 0 orders**, or if spend per order is over **$45** |
| **B. Meta boost** of the best organic print Reel, Bay Area + US interests in home decor and nature photography, linking to the print page with `utm_medium=paid` | **$5/day × 7 days = $35 max** | Cost per landing-page view; Shopify add-to-carts and orders | Stop after **$15** if a landing-page view costs **more than $1.00**; stop at $35 with 0 add-to-carts |

- **Total cap: about $77.** That's under one framed 12x18's keep, so a single sale pays for the whole test.
- **Don't run** TikTok Promote, LinkedIn or Google Ads for prints in this stage.

### Stage 2: Q1 2027, only if Stage 1 returns a sale at a CPA of $45 or less
- **Pinterest ads** on product pins (needs the catalog/tag [P1]): **$5–10/day for 30 days** ($150–300), the same $45 CPA kill rule, and a review each Monday.
- Or **scale the winner from Stage 1** by 2×, keeping the same kill rule.

### Events: no paid
A single event booking is worth more than a year of print ads, and it comes from outreach and referrals (REVENUE-ENGINE.md levers 1–2). Revisit paid search for "event photographer San Francisco" only after the Google Business Profile has reviews. That's already a backlog research item.
