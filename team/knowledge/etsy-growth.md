## 1. How Etsy search ranks in 2026, and what it means for these 39 listings

Etsy's own explanation, in [How Etsy Search Works](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461474487) (Etsy, "as of August 2025"; still the current version on 2026-10-26), has two phases:

1. **Query matching.** Etsy looks at the title, tags, attributes, description, **first photo**, reviews and more. Its advice is to use all 13 tags, vary the tags, add every relevant attribute, and pick the most specific category.
2. **Ranking:**

| Factor (Etsy's wording) | What Etsy says | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Relevancy | Matches in tags, titles, descriptions, categories and attributes. **Exact matches may rank higher.** | Titles and tags are solid (from the CSV). **Attributes look empty** (see §2.1). That is the biggest relevancy gap. |
| Shop quality | "Your shop should have a shop icon"; a complete shop profile matters. Etsy says an incomplete shop may hurt the visibility of its listings ([search visibility page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/25869947521175), unverified summary). | Icon and banner are set. About is one line, and there's no shop title, sections, announcement or FAQ. |
| Listing quality | Multiple high-quality photos and a return policy. **Shipping price** is a factor for US sellers. | About 9 photos per listing and free shipping are good. A return policy is set, but it says "not accepted" (see §2.6). |
| Customer service | Average review rating, message response rate and **case rate over the last 3 months** ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/375461474487)). Great reviews and an About section can help ranking; IP issues and cases hurt it ([Etsy handbook index](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/the-ultimate-guide-to-etsy-search/366469415790)). | We have 0 reviews. The first 5–10 orders matter a lot. Avoid cases by shipping on time (see the processing-time risk in §2.6). |
| Listing engagement rate | Etsy measures how well a listing converts: views to purchases. | Conversion is the lever we control: first photo, price anchor, description and reviews. |
| Recency | New and renewed listings get a small boost that lasts "a few hours to a few days". Etsy says renewing just for the boost "isn't an effective" strategy. | The launch boost is already spent. Don't renew for rank, and stagger batch 2 and batch 3 to get fresh learning periods. |
| Shoppers' habits (Context Specific Ranking) | Results are personalised, so searching your own listings won't show your rank. | Judge rank from Stats → traffic → search terms, never from manual searches. |
| Star Seller | **Not a direct ranking factor**, but a trust signal that can lift conversion. | It needs 95% replies within 24 hours, 95% on-time shipping with tracking, a 4.8+ rating, and a clean case rate ([Etsy Help: Star Seller vs standards](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/29654393638679)). It can't happen until there are orders. |
| Etsy Plus / Etsy Ads | **Neither** affects organic placement. | Ads buy ad slots only (§3). |

- **Attributes** power the sidebar filters. Etsy says a listing can appear in filtered results **only if it has that attribute**. Attributes also feed recipient and seasonal shopping pages ([Etsy: Add attributes](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/604203126614)).
  - Wall-art shoppers filter heavily by orientation, room, framing, size and colour.
  - Empty attributes therefore cut us out of filtered searches entirely.
- **Search visibility page** (Shop Manager → Marketing or Stats → Search visibility): Etsy's per-shop checklist for listings, shop and customer service, with suggested actions ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/25869947521175), unverified summary). Check it weekly. It's the quickest way to see what Etsy itself thinks is missing.

### 2.6 Policies and shipping risks

1. **Processing time (urgent, Logan).** A next-day delivery estimate on framed prints means Etsy will set a ship-by date Gelato can't meet.
   - Late shipments count against on-time shipping. The customer service standard is 80%, Star Seller is 95%, and falling short "may lower your search visibility" ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/29654393638679)).
   - **Fix:** set the processing profiles to Gelato's real production time. Check the Gelato dashboard for framed and for posters. A reasonable guess is 2–5 business days for framed and 1–3 for paper (unverified).
   - Also check "Ships from San Francisco". Gelato routes US orders to its own partner printers, so the origin ZIP drives Etsy's delivery estimate.
2. **Returns "not accepted".** That's allowed, and Etsy Purchase Protection still covers damaged items. Shoppers see it on every listing, though, so state the damage and replacement promise in the FAQ and descriptions (§2.4).
3. **Cancellations within 1 hour.** Fine for made-to-order; Gelato starts production quickly.

## 3. Etsy Ads

**How they work** ([Etsy Help: set up Etsy Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033701174)):
- **Pay per click.** Ads run in Etsy search, category and market pages, in buyers' countries only.
- **15-day wait** after opening a shop. The shop went live about 2026-10-23, so ads are available from about **Nov 7**. Shop Manager shows a countdown.
- **Budget:**
  - The daily budget is a hard cap. The minimum is $1/day (from search summaries; unverified).
  - Every seller starts with a **$25/day maximum**, recalculated weekly.
  - Below a $25 max budget, every listing uses the "Efficient spending" strategy.
- **All listings are advertised by default** when you start, so deselect the ones you don't want.
- Up to 20 **ad groups**. Etsy recommends running a strategy for **at least 30 days** before judging it.
- Etsy's own caution: ads "are unlikely to turn a low-demand listing into a top seller". Optimise the listings first.
- If the last click before a sale is an Etsy Ad, only the Etsy Ads cost applies, not the Offsite Ads fee ([Etsy Help: Offsite Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367)).

**Margins and break-even ROAS.**
- Assumptions: Gelato cost ≈ site price ÷ 2.5; Etsy fees ≈ 9.5% + $0.45; free shipping.
- Contribution = Etsy price − Gelato cost − fees. Break-even ROAS = price ÷ contribution.

| Listing | Etsy price | Gelato cost | Fees | Contribution | **Break-even ROAS** | Max ad cost per sale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 8x12 | $39 | $14 | $4.16 | $20.84 | 1.87 | $20.84 |
| Paper 24x36 | $79 | $28 | $7.96 | $43.04 | 1.84 | $43.04 |
| Framed 8x12 | $129 | $46 | $12.70 | $70.30 | 1.84 | $70.30 |
| Framed 12x18 | $175 | $62 | $17.07 | $95.93 | 1.82 | $95.93 |
| Framed 16x24 | $229 | $82 | $22.20 | $124.80 | 1.83 | $124.80 |
| Framed 24x36 | $355 | $126 | $34.18 | $194.82 | 1.82 | $194.82 |

- Break-even is a **ROAS of about 1.85** at every price.
- Ad cost per sale is roughly CPC ÷ conversion rate. Click costs aren't published. Illustrative only: $0.40 ÷ 1.5% ≈ $27 per sale (CPC figure from [listingview](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-etsy-ads-work-in-2026-and-when-to-turn-them-off), third-party).
- At that rate a $39 paper sale loses money, while a framed sale keeps $43 or more.
- **So advertise framed listings, not paper.**

**Recommended test (Logan sets the amount; BUSINESS.md allows a small holiday print-ad test):**
- **Budget:** **$3/day**, Nov 7 – Dec 13. That's about 37 days and **at most about $110**.
- **Stop date:** stop on Dec 13, ahead of the Gelato framed cut-off (§4.5).
- **Listings:** the **framed** listings for the 8 strongest search terms:
  - International Orange
  - Leviathan's Leap
  - Rainier Afterglow
  - Drift and Timber
  - Valley in Violet
  - Sierra Snowline
  - Salt and Scales
  - Obsidian Palace
- **Turn ads off** on all 19 paper listings and the other 12 framed listings.
- Put the advertised listings in one ad group, "Holiday framed test".

**Kill and scale rules** (my heuristics, built on Etsy's 30-day guidance; the ROAS ladder matches third-party advice, [listingview](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-etsy-ads-work-in-2026-and-when-to-turn-them-off)):

| When | Signal | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Any time | A listing has spent **$15 with 0 orders and under 2 favourites** | Turn off ads on that listing and fix its photo or title |
| Any time | A listing's click-through rate is under 1% after 1,000 ad views | Change the first photo first, then decide |
| Day 30 | Ad-group ROAS **under 1.85** | Stop. It's losing money. |
| Day 30 | ROAS **1.85–3.0** | Hold the budget and swap in the next 2 listings for the 2 weakest |
| Day 30 or Cyber Week | ROAS **over 3.0** | Raise the budget by 50% (e.g. $3 to $4.50), but not above what Logan has approved |
| Dec 13 | Last safe framed order date approaching | Pause all ads |

**Offsite Ads.**
- **Terms** ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367)):
  - 15% of an attributed order, charged only on a sale within 30 days of an ad click, capped at $100 per order.
  - The shop can opt out while it has never made $10k in any 365-day period.
  - Etsy picks which listings it advertises: on Google, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, Bing and partner sites.
- **My recommendation: stay opted in through the holidays, then review in January.** This matches the launch plan.
  - It's profitable at every price. The worst case, paper 8x12, still nets about $15 (launch plan, §2).
  - It costs nothing unless a sale happens.
  - A new shop with 0 reviews has almost no other reach.
- **Opt out in January if both of these hold:**
  - more than half of orders are Offsite-attributed
  - organic Etsy search traffic is growing on its own
- **Why that rule:** Offsite attribution then mostly taxes buyers who would have found the shop anyway. It also overrides Share & Save, because the last click wins ([Etsy Help: Share & Save](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16981332744087)).
- **If the manager would rather opt out now:** the cost is lost reach, not money. It's a defensible choice for Logan to make.

## 4. Sales, coupons, Share & Save and holiday timing

Source for tools: [Etsy Help: Sales and discounts](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014260108). The tools are:
- promo codes, from 5% to 75% off or a fixed amount, with optional usage limits and one use per buyer
- timed sales
- order-minimum discounts
- bundles of up to 3 listings
- **targeted offers:** four automatic emails, sent only to shoppers who opted in to marketing email:
  - abandoned cart
  - favourited item
  - interested shopper
  - thank you

**Discount floor.**
- A framed 8x12 stays above Gelato cost plus fees up to about **60% off**, or about 52% off on an Offsite-attributed sale.
- **Cap every discount at 25%.** At that level a framed 8x12 still nets about $41, and paper 8x12 about $12.
- Any code that drops a price under Gelato cost loses money (BUSINESS.md rule).

### 4.1 Targeted offers (set up once, 10% each)

| Offer | Discount | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Abandoned cart | 10% | Recovers buyers who hesitated on the $129+ framed price |
| Favourited item | 10% | Favourites are the earliest demand signal we'll get |
| Thank you (next order) | 10% | Repeat buyers, e.g. a gallery-wall second print |
| Interested shopper | 10% | Same logic. Turn it off if it fires too often |

10% off a $129 framed print still nets about $58. Etsy may change the timing and frequency of these emails ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014260108)).

### 4.2 Sales

- **Don't** copy the competitors' permanent "25% off, everything on sale" pattern (§6). Etsy reminds sellers they must follow consumer-protection law on sales ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014260108)). A fake reference price is the main risk.
- **Do** run one real **Cyber Week sale: 15% off framed listings, Fri Nov 27 – Mon Nov 30, 2026** (Cyber Monday falls on Nov 30 in 2026). Keep paper out of it.
  - At 15% off, the Etsy framed price is roughly the site price, so the site isn't undercut.
- **Bundle:** "Salt and Stone, any 2 framed", 10% off. Bundles are capped at 3 listings, so a set of 3 also works.

### 4.3 Share & Save (Logan enrols)

- **What it pays:** 4% back on orders placed within 30 days of a click on Logan's own trackable links. **From Oct 20 to Jan 4, Etsy is paying 6.5%** ([Etsy Help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16981332744087), read 2026-10-26).
- **Links that count:** the shop link (`oxhollowmediaprints.etsy.com`), listing links (Listings → gear → Share), promo-code links, and, in the app, section and review links.
- **Links that don't count:** links sent in Etsy Messages or placed in listing descriptions.
- **What it means:**
  - During the promo, an Etsy order through Logan's link on a framed 12x18 nets about $95.93 + 6.5% × $175 = **about $107**, against **$88** on the site.
  - So through Jan 4, sending Instagram print traffic to the Etsy listing nets more *and* earns the reviews the shop needs.
  - That conflicts with the standing rule that Instagram links to the site. **It's Logan's call.**
  - My suggestion: use Etsy listing links in Stories and posts for the 8 advertised prints until Jan 4, and keep the site as the bio link.

## 5. First reviews (ethically) and outside traffic

### Reviews

- **How reviews work:** buyers have **100 days** after delivery to review. The shop score is a recency-weighted average in which a rating's weight halves each year ([Etsy Help: review system](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000572708)).
- **Allowed:**
  - one short, neutral post-delivery message, e.g. "If you have a moment, a review helps a small shop. Thank you."
  - great packaging and fast replies
  - fixing problems quickly so they never become cases
- **Not allowed:**
  - any discount, freebie or refund in exchange for a review
  - asking for a specific star rating
  - friends or family buying in order to review
- **Policy sources:** third-party summaries of Etsy's seller policy and anti-shilling rules, and of the FTC's Consumer Reviews Rule (2024): [listingview](https://listingview.io/blogs/how-to-get-more-reviews-on-etsy-without-breaking-the-rules), [printify](https://printify.com/blog/etsy-reviews/). I couldn't open Etsy's own reviews policy page, so this is **unverified against Etsy's text**. Treat it as a hard rule anyway.
- **Real buyers Logan knows:** if people from Logan's network genuinely want a print, they can buy one. Don't ask them to review. Under the FTC rule and Etsy's shilling rules, reviews from close connections are risky.
- **Speed:** the quickest honest route to the first reviews is to send the Instagram audience who already likes the work to specific listings (Share & Save, §4.3).

### Outside traffic

- **Instagram:**
  - bio: keep the site link (team rule) and add "Etsy shop" as a second link with the Share & Save shop link
