Case study

Ocean Defenders: a field film for a marine nonprofit

2023 · 9:28 · Pro bono, for Ocean Defenders Alliance

A story of contention to camaraderie: how Kurt Lieber, founder of Ocean Defenders Alliance, found partnership with fishermen throughout Southern California to recover lost fishing gear, return it to its owners and promote ocean health.

Goal

Tell the story of Ocean Defenders Alliance's partnership with Southern California fishermen in a way the public, partners and supporters can feel.

Approach

I directed, shot, edited and produced the film as pro bono work for the organization.

Delivered

A 9:28 documentary, published on Vimeo in June 2023.
