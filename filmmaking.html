Questions

What kinds of films do you make?
Short documentaries, impact films for nonprofits, and brand films for ocean and outdoor companies.

Yes. Concept, interviews, filming on land and on the water, editing, color, and sound.
How much does a film cost?
Films start at $6,500. Most land between $8,000 and $20,000, depending on shoot days, travel, crew, and licensing.

I’m based in the San Francisco Bay Area and film across California and the West Coast. Travel is quoted per project.
