# 90-day growth plan (2026-10-27 → 2027-01-24)

**Goal:** turn @oxhollowmedia from about 67 followers with little reach into a steady source of print traffic and event credibility, with $0 spend, about 1.5 hours a week of Logan's time, and Pinterest as a second, evergreen print channel.

## The 5 highest-leverage moves

1. **One story Reel a week built for sends and watch time, led by Salt and Stone.**
   - Obsidian Palace is the proven hook. Make it a series (Phase Shift, Divine Duality, then new abstracts), alternating with Galápagos 2019 and Eastern Sierra.
   - Reels: 12–20 s, hook in the first 2 s, place + subject in the first caption line, 3–5 hashtags, location tag.
   - Use Trial Reels as soon as the toggle appears (eligibility unclear at 67 followers).
   - *Why:* watch time and sends drive non-follower reach; carousels drive saves. (instagram-growth §1–2)
2. **Pinterest set up properly in November and fed 10 fresh pins a week.**
   - Business account, claimed site, Rich Pins (print pages already have product markup), Etsy connected, 8 search-named boards, scheduled in Metricool.
   - *Why:* decor is Pinterest's biggest category and pins last for months. Starting before the holidays begins the 3–6 month ramp. (pinterest-tiktok)
3. **One collab post a month with a named past client or partner.**
   - Ocean Defenders Alliance, Conservation Made Simple, Illuminate Conservation and the others named in BUSINESS.md, with people's consent. Logan sends the invite.
   - *Why:* one post reaches up to 5 audiences, and conservation partners lend credibility, as in every large account in the teardown.
4. **The 10-minute engagement routine, 4 days a week, plus one "original vs edited" post a month.**
   - Replies, 3 real comments on small local accounts, one genuine send.
   - The RAW-vs-final post shows craft and is unmistakably human, which is what Instagram's 2026 originality push rewards.
5. **$0 ads until two triggers fire, then one capped holiday test.**
   - Keep Etsy Offsite Ads on (pay only on a sale).
   - If triggered: Etsy Ads $3/day for 14 days and/or a $35 Meta boost of the best organic Reel, about $77 total, stopping at a CPA over $45. Logan sets the amount. (ad-budget)
   - TikTok: mirror the same MP4s through Metricool, with no new production. Review on day 45.
