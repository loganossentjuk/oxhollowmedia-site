# General liability insurance options (researched 2026-11-05)

Why: venues and corporate clients ask for a certificate of insurance (COI), usually $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate, often naming them as additional insured. Without it Logan loses bookings where the venue asks. Prints and films don't need it.

| Option | Price (as published; verify at quote) | GL limit | Gear cover | COI / additional insured | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Full Frame Insurance, Annual** | $129/yr ($12/mo) | up to $2M/$2M | optional add-on | unlimited additional insureds on event policies; COI process not stated on site | Cheapest annual; photographer-specific |
| **Full Frame, Annual+** | $347/yr ($30.17/mo) | $2M | up to $75k camera gear | as above | Best if gear cover wanted in one policy |
| **Full Frame, Event** | $59 per 1–3 days | up to $2M | no | unlimited additional insureds | Per-event, if bookings stay rare |
| **Thimble** | by the hour/day/month; quote only | typically $1M/$2M | separate | COI instant after purchase; venues use its Certificate Manager | Per-event flexibility; Thimble COIs are widely accepted |
| **PPA Full Plus membership** | $428/yr | GL via Lockton Affinity (limits on member page, 403 to us) | PhotoCare equipment plan is a member benefit | via Lockton | Bundles a trade membership; pricier than Full Frame for GL alone |
| **NEXT / Hiscox / Simply Business** | ~$17–29/mo medians nationally; Insureon puts CA photographers ≈$425/yr | $1M/$2M typical | add-on | instant COIs (NEXT) | General small-business carriers; fine, nothing photo-specific |

Not verified directly (pages 403/404 to the fetcher): PPA Full Plus GL limits; Thimble day price. Numbers above are from carrier pages and 2026 guides; a real quote depends on revenue and gear value.

## Recommendation
Full Frame **Annual** ($129/yr) now, naming venues as additional insured per event; move to **Annual+** when gear value justifies it. If Logan wants per-event only until bookings are steady: Full Frame Event ($59) or Thimble per day. Either way, add the COI line to the quote template ("COI naming the venue available on request").
