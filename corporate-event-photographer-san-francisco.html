FAQ

Answers, up front.

How much does a corporate event photographer cost in San Francisco?
Mid-level SF event photographers run roughly $225–350/hour in 2026. My coverage starts at $1,650 for a half day and $3,200 for a full day, gallery included.
How fast will we get our photos?
A same-week highlight set for social, and the complete edited gallery within 5 business days. Rush and same-day social edits are available.
Do you photograph multi-day conferences?
Yes — from $6,500, scoped to your run-of-show, with an optional second shooter for parallel tracks.
Which venues and areas do you cover?
All of San Francisco and the greater Bay Area — Moscone, Fort Mason, The Midway, Pier 27, hotels, offices, and private venues — plus Oakland, San Jose, and the Peninsula. Travel beyond is quoted separately.
