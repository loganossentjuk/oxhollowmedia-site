# Outreach playbook (rules for every cold email)

Source: `research-tech-events-and-reels.md` (2026-10-28). Logan's 90-day priority is **tech-company events**. Framing is mixed by audience: offer-led for tech and planners, mission-led for nonprofits and science.

## What went wrong in the 2026-10-28 batch
The batch went to info@ inboxes and opened with "I'm Logan, an event photographer". The middle listed turnaround times, and the close asked them to "pass my name along" or "send a rate sheet". Nothing in it was specific to the reader, there was no single proof image, and the ask cost them effort. Don't repeat any of this.

## Who
- **One named person** per company, at most two. Target 50–500-person companies.
  - Event or field marketing, community/DevRel, brand.
  - People/Workplace for offsites.
  - The founder at small startups.
- **Find them on public pages only:** event pages, speaker and sponsor lists, team pages, LinkedIn profiles. Never guess an email address.
  - If you can't confirm a named person's email, draft for the company's contact form and give Logan the person's name and LinkedIn URL so he can connect there.
- **Warmest route first:** introductions through past clients (Semgrep), then venue preferred-vendor lists, then cold email.

## The email (50–90 words, plain text)
1. **Subject:** 2–5 lowercase words that name their thing, e.g. `your jan kickoff`, `photos for devday`.
2. **Line 1:** one specific trigger. Examples: their upcoming event or offsite, a recent launch or funding round, a conference they're sponsoring, the venue they use. Never open with "I'm Logan".
3. **Line 2:** one line of proof. Example: "I shot Semgrep's offsite and conference" plus **one** link to the single most relevant gallery or recap, not the whole site.
4. **Line 3:** the outcome, not a feature list. Example: "a short recap reel cut for LinkedIn within 48 hours, photos the same week". Photo and video come in one booking.
5. **Close:** a yes/no interest question. Examples: "Worth a look for the January offsite?" or "Is that useful for you?" Never ask for a call, a rate-sheet request or a referral in the first email.
6. **Signature:**
   ```
   Logan Ossentjuk · Ox Hollow Media
   oxhollowmedia.com · 2139 Turk Blvd, San Francisco, CA 94115
   Reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.
   ```
   The address and the opt-out line are both CAN-SPAM requirements.

**Approved lines (Logan, 2026-10-28). Use these exact words, one per email:**
- **Tech** (one line, after the proof): "Illuminating the unseen: the moments and people that show who your company really is."
- **Nonprofits, science and climate groups** (opening, as the mission): "Striving to bring people closer to nature, illuminating stories of action, and belief in conservation, cultivation and care."
- **Planners and venues** (closing note): "I capture the moments and the feeling of your event so they last long after the night ends."
- Keep NOAA out of all Ox Hollow outreach; that role is separate.

**Mission line placement:**
- **Tech:** at most one short clause, e.g. "I'm also an ocean and climate storyteller, so I care about getting people's stories right".
- **Nonprofits and science groups, People/Workplace teams and climate-tech companies:** lead with it.
- Draft lines for each audience are in `research-tech-events-and-reels.md` §3. They're visible copy, so use only lines Logan has approved.

**Voice:** use Logan's own voice (the `my-writing-style` skill). No hype, exclamation marks, em dashes or emoji. Every fact must come from team/BUSINESS.md.

## Follow-ups
- Send two short replies in the same thread: around day 3–4 (one new detail or a different image) and around day 10 (the "who owns event content?" question). Then stop.
- If someone replies "no thanks", log it and never contact them again.

## Timing and volume
- **When to pitch:**
  - Late Oct–Dec: January–March kickoffs and offsites.
  - February: the best reply month, for Q2 conferences.
  - June–August: Dreamforce-week work.
- **When to send:** Wed–Thu, 8am–noon in the recipient's time zone.
- **Volume:** 10 a week, sent by Logan. Turn open/click tracking off.
- **Deliverability:** a custom-domain sender (hello@oxhollowmedia.com) roughly doubles replies compared with a free Gmail address. Setting up Gmail "send as" with SPF and DKIM is on Logan's list.

## The offer: The Recap (Logan, 2026-10-28)
- **What it is:** up to 2 hours of coverage, about 100–150 edited photos within the week, and a 30–60 s recap reel within 48 hours.
- **Price:** **$1,200** standard; **$1,000 for a first booking**.
- **In first emails:** mention it as "a first-booking recap package". Give the price when they show interest or reply, or in follow-up 2.

## Mix (from 2026-10-28, 90 days)
- 6 tech events: in-house event, community and People teams, plus agencies and venues that serve tech.
- 2 nonprofit/science films, mission-led.
- 2 prints: designers, offices, gift guides.
