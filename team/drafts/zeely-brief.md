# Zeely brief: Ox Hollow Media print ads

For Logan to paste into Zeely (Brand space + prompt fields). Drafted 2026-10-16. Facts come only from `team/BUSINESS.md` and `prints/catalog.json`.

## 1. Hard rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Real photos only.** Upload only my own print files: `Dropbox/Career/OxHollow/PrintMasters/gelato-ready/HighResolution/` (masters) or `images/portfolio/` in the site repo. Never use a Zeely stock or AI-generated photo as "the print". The coast and misty-valley ads are not in the catalog, so delete them.
2. **Zeely never touches the photo.** It can render the room, wall, hands and frame around it. It can't extend, recolour, "enhance", crop the subject, or add or remove anything inside the image. If the print in the mockup doesn't match the original, throw it out.
3. **Only formats I sell for that print.** Framed print (black, white, oak), framed canvas (black, oak, walnut) and paper. Wood and acrylic only where the catalog lists them (Obsidian Palace and International Orange do; Into the Unknown, Highland Hollow and Shark Shadow don't). **No plain gallery-wrap canvas.** It's archived.
4. **Moon lockup only.** `brand/logos/oxhollow-lockup-green.png` (on light backgrounds) or `brand/logos/oxhollow-lockup-dark.png`; moon mark alone: `brand/logos/moon.svg`. Never use the old mountain mark (`oxhollow-legacy-mark.jpeg`) or plain "Ox Hollow Media" type.
5. **Prices from the catalog only.** Paper from $35, framed print from $115, framed canvas from $125. Don't put sale prices, "% off" or made-up bundles in an ad.
6. **No fake proof.** No reviews, star ratings, "bestseller", "limited edition", "museum quality", award badges or customer counts. The shop has no orders yet.
7. **Files and watermark.** Upload the gelato-ready master to Zeely (the web copies in `images/portfolio/` carry a watermark that looks wrong inside a frame). Export ads at screen size only, and never post or share the full-res file.
8. **People.** Only show a recognisable face (a real person, not an AI one) if they've given their OK.

## 3. Scene direction

**Use:** calm rooms with natural window light; wood, linen, plaster and stone; one print per wall with plenty of empty space around it; hands holding the frame; outdoor POV scenes like the tent one (a cabin porch, a car tailgate at dawn, a trailhead bench). Muted, warm colour.
**Avoid:** glossy luxury penthouses, marble-and-gold rooms, busy gallery walls, neon, sticker badges, arrows, starbursts, "SALE" banners and stacked text blocks. Never put the print on an easel or any product I don't sell.
