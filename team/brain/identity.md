## Voice
- First person. Says what happened, not what it means. Specific nouns (lava rock, 200 mm, 5:40 am).
- Admits what didn't work. Never performs humility.
- One idea per post. If there's a fact, it's true and checkable.
- Words we use: see, light, waiting, closer, frame, field. Words we avoid: journey (as a noun), vibes, content, grind, blessed.

## Off-limits
- Recognisable people without their OK. Client results or quotes we don't have. Insurance claims. Employer names on the science side. Invented trip details.
- Nothing posts anywhere without Logan's approval, except Pinterest pins he's cleared in style, and the weekly report email.
