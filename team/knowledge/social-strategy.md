# Social strategy (approved direction, Oct 28 2026)

Logan's answers: core identity = **the ocean storyteller**, expressed differently per platform. 90-day goal = **audience size**. On camera = text only for now (recommendation below). Every draft is judged in this order: **1. Distribution** (hook under 12 words, instantly clear, shareable) → **2. Qualification** (pulls nature lovers, wall-art buyers, Bay Area tech/event people, not empty traffic) → **3. Positioning** (strengthens "ocean and wild places, seen up close" and Ox Hollow's authority).

## Positioning
- One line: **Ox Hollow shows you the parts of the ocean and wild places most people never see.** ("Dare to see." / "Illuminating the unseen.")
- Mission underneath: bringing people closer to nature; conservation, cultivation and care.
- What makes it ownable: real places (Galápagos 2019, Oahu's Mermaid's Pools, Eastern Sierra, Golden Gate), abstract ocean work (Salt and Stone), and the fact that every frame is a print you can hang.

## Audience psychology
| Who | What they want from a scroll | What makes them follow |
|---|---|---|
| Nature and ocean lovers (largest pool) | Awe, calm, "I didn't know that" | A reliable feed of wonder plus one surprising fact |
| Wall-art and home buyers (Pinterest, Etsy) | A room that feels like them | Seeing the print framed, in a room, at a price |
| Photographers and creators | How it was made | Process, settings, before/after |
| Bay Area tech and event people | Proof of craft and taste | Polished, human moments; local places they know |

## Platform roles
- **Instagram:** polished home base. Carousels (photobooks), Reels, and Stories. Collabs with organisers after events.
- **TikTok:** raw and curious. Same Reels re-posted by Logan with a trending sound added in the app, plus "guess what this is" and fact-led cuts.
- **Pinterest:** search engine for wall art. 1 pin a day, framed mockup on cream, keyword titles, links to print pages.

## Growth pillars (rotate weekly)
1. **Wonder + one fact** (reach): an animal or place plus one true, surprising line. *"This lizard sneezes salt."*
2. **Guess / reveal** (watch time, comments): detail → full frame → framed print → end card.
3. **Original vs edited** (shares, saves among creators): raw frame → finished print.
4. **Collections as photobooks** (saves, profile visits): Galápagos, Eastern Sierra, Salt and Stone.
5. **Behind the shot** (follows): where, when, what it took. Needs Logan's own details, never invented.

## Face or text?
Text-only works for reach on wonder content, but **people follow people**: personality-led clips are what TikTok rewards and what turns a viewer into a follower. Recommended path: **start with voiceover** (record 10–20 seconds on the phone, no face) on 1–2 posts a week, keep the rest text-only, and compare follows per 1,000 views after 4 weeks. Add face only if voice wins.

## Cadence (fits 1–2 hours a week of Logan's time)
- Instagram: 3 Reels + 1 carousel a week (team builds, Logan approves).
- TikTok: Logan re-posts the Instagram Reels with a trending sound.
- Pinterest: 1 pin a day, scheduled in batches.
- Engagement: reply to every comment within 24 hours in the first hour after posting if possible; comment on 5 accounts in the niche per post day.

## Metrics that matter (weekly report)
Follows per 1,000 views, saves and shares per post, profile visits, link clicks to /prints (UTM), and which pillar won.
