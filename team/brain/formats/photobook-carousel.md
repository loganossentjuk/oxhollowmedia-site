# Photobook carousel
**Account:** @oxhollowmedia · **Pillar:** proof · **Length:** 6–10 slides · **Status:** in use

## Structure
Cover (title over photo) → title page (collection, place) → 3–6 spreads (one photo per slide, cream margins, title) → back page (prints, link in bio).
## Hook pattern
First slide is the strongest photo with the place name. Caption: one sentence on the place, one on the frames, "Link in bio."
## What Logan must supply
Approval. Optional: one line of memory per spread.
## Build
`scripts/make_photobook.py` from `book.json` (see Dropbox Instagram Drafts folders).
