## 3. Four-week posting plan (Reels first, plus the carousel)

**Format and slots:**
- Three Reels and one carousel, one series post a week.
- Each Reel takes the Wednesday 12:00 pm print slot in `team/drafts/instagram-plan.md`. That slot is in Buffer's top three [S4] and Sprout's Wednesday window [S3]. The Galápagos Reel moves back a week.
- The carousel goes in Tuesday's 1:00 pm slot [S3].
- Re-check all slots against Metricool's heatmap once it's connected.

**Trial Reels:** turn Trial on for each Reel. It shows the Reel to non-followers first [S5].

**Hashtags:** 3 to 5 per post, because Instagram caps posts at 5 [S1].

**Audio:** add it in the app only. Pick a trending sound on the day, with slow ambient as the fallback. Never put music in the file.

**Comments:** reply to every "What do you see?" answer.

**Photos:** the portfolio JPGs carry the Ox Hollow mark (slides 1 and 4). Re-export them from your originals if you want them clean.

- for Reels: views [IGRE23] and 3-second view rate [IGRE28]

**Ratio to compare:** shares plus saves per 1,000 reached, against the Obsidian Palace photo post.

**GA4.**
- **Visits:** Reports › Acquisition › Traffic acquisition, filtered to session campaign `abstract-series`, split by session manual ad content (`utm_content`) to see which post drove visits.
- **Checkouts:** the `begin_checkout` event count for the same campaign.

**Shopify.** `js/main.js` keeps `utm_campaign` for the session and adds `ref=abstract-series` to every cart link. Orders from the series show that ref.

**Decision after week 4:** whichever hook earned the most shares and saves per reach becomes the template for the next abstracts.
