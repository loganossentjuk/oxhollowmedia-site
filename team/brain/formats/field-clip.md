# Field clip
**Account:** @oxhollowmedia · **Pillar:** in the field · **Length:** 15–40 s · **Status:** not yet tried (needs footage)

## Structure
1. 0–2 s: Logan's phone clip, mid-action, voice: where we are and what we're waiting for.
2. 2–15 s: the wait, the attempt, the miss or the moment. Cut on the beat.
3. 15–25 s: the frame it produced (or "didn't get it; here's what I got instead").
4. Last 3 s: one line of what he learned. No end card; just the handle.
## Hook pattern
Voice-first: "It's 5:40 and the iguanas aren't here." / "I flew 4,000 miles for this and it's raining."
## What Logan must supply
Phone clips (10–30 s, vertical, talk to camera or narrate after). One per outing is enough.
## Build
Manual cut by design-specialist; captions burned in (cream band), sound added by Logan in-app.
