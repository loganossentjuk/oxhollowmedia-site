FAQ

Answers, up front.

Do you photograph nonprofit galas and fundraisers in San Francisco?
Yes. Galas, benefit dinners, auctions, and donor receptions across San Francisco and the Bay Area, with a same-week highlight set for your thank-you emails and social posts.
Can you capture both photos and video at our event?
Yes. I shoot stills and film, so one booking can cover your gallery plus a short recap film or donor-appeal video. Combined photo and film coverage is scoped to your program.
Do you work with environmental and conservation organizations?
That is the heart of my work. My documentary shorts follow ocean cleanup and endangered-species conservation, and I bring the same approach to your field days, cleanups, and community events.
How much does nonprofit event photography cost?
Coverage starts at $1,650 for up to 4 hours and $3,200 for a full day, with a color-graded gallery delivered within 5 business days. Tell me about your budget and program and I will build a quote around it.
