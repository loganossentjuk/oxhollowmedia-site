Selected work

Logan Ossentjuk

I am a photographer, videographer and science storyteller based in the San Francisco Bay Area. I have been shooting since 2017. I make short documentaries about conservation, and I photograph and film events for companies and nonprofits. I run this work as Ox Hollow Media.

Below are two films, a few frames from recent events, and the people I have worked with.

Conservation films

I directed, shot, edited and produced both. Each one follows people doing hard, patient work for a place they care about.

Ocean Defenders

2023 · 9:28 · Pro bono, for Ocean Defenders Alliance

A story of contention to camaraderie, how Kurt Lieber, founder of Ocean Defenders Alliance, found partnership with fishermen throughout Southern California to recover lost fishing gear, return it to its owners and promote ocean health.

Brushstrokes For Survival

2023 · 14:35

Driven to make a difference, Nate Irwin, a conservationist turned artist partners with his father, cascading paint on canvas to illuminate the plight of critically endangered steelhead in the Santa Ynez River Valley and cultivate action towards their conservation.
