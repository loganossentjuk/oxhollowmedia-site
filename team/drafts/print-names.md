## Naming principles

- **"Dare to see."** Name what the eye actually lands on (the light, the texture, the moment), not a mood. No hype words.
- **Ox Hollow = "little hollow" + "oxen yoke".** Lean on the hollow and the valley where the photo really has one (Highland Hollow). Don't force it anywhere else.
- **"Bring people closer to nature"** (Instagram bio). Plain nouns from the frame: fog, flats, scrub, stone, spark. If the place or species is known, name it (Fitz Roy, Rainier, Sierra, Golden Gate, blue-footed booby). If it isn't, leave it out.
- **"Every scale of the natural world"** (about.html, Photography) and **"Three ways of seeing"**: series group small studies (Embers, Petal Portraits) so the big scenes can stand alone.
- **Alliteration only where it reads aloud naturally**, about one title in three. Short: 1 to 4 words, no em dashes. Comma form ("Petunia, Crimson") stays for series members.
- **No clashes.** No two titles share a lead word. Searchable species names stay in the title. Spelling is US (Harbor, not Harbour).
