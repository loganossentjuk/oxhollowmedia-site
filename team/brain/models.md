## Borrowed structure for @oxhollowmedia
- **Bio:** "Ocean and wildlife photographer, SF Bay Area / Science background. Shooting since 2017. Events fund the ocean work. / Prints and bookings ↓". No "aspiring", no "Nat Geo".
- **Pinned (3):** one-minute best-of Reel ("Shooting since 2017. Here is where it got me."); face-to-camera "why I left the lab for this" (Logan writes it); "Every Galápagos species I've photographed" carousel ending on prints.
- **Rotation:** Mon serial-subject Reel · Wed teach-one-thing · Fri finished frame with the story and one ask · every other Sat a climb log. Stories only when in the field.
- **Platform facts:** Mosseri: no fixed cadence, keep a sustainable pattern; studies cluster at 3–5 posts/week.
