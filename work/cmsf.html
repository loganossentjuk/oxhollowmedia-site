Case study

Ocean Defenders

2023 · 9:28 · Pro bono, for Ocean Defenders Alliance

A story of contention to camaraderie: how Kurt Lieber, founder of Ocean Defenders Alliance, found partnership with fishermen throughout Southern California to recover lost fishing gear, return it to its owners and promote ocean health.

Goal

Help Ocean Defenders Alliance reach Southern California fishers and harbor communities about lost fishing gear, and share its partnership with fishermen with a wider public.

Approach

As Outreach Manager for Debris Free Seas, a UC Santa Barbara Bren School project with Ocean Defenders Alliance as our client, I led a bilingual outreach campaign shaped by a community survey of 300+ responses and more than 10 expert interviews. I directed, filmed, edited, and produced the documentary, filming from the boats alongside the crews.

Delivered

Bilingual English and Spanish flyers, stickers, and survey materials; the ODA style guide and one-pager; a ghost-gear hot-spot map and data webpage; a public talk; and the 9:28 documentary, published in June 2023.

Brand and voice

Guiding Group Behaviors

Rebranded and rewrote NOAA Office for Coastal Management's national facilitation curriculum, turning "Dealing with Disruptive Behaviors" into Guiding Group Behaviors with a strengths-based voice grounded in psychological safety and dignity. I wrote nine archetypes and produced new slide decks, handouts, web content, and reference materials used nationally.

Storytelling

Co-created with NOAA Instructional Design Team, a storytelling module that teaches coastal managers to collect community stories and bring them into decision-making. TEAM Spotlight Award, 2026.
