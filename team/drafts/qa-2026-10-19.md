# Weekly QA, 2026-10-19 (report only, no site files edited)

Branch: team/week-2026-10-19. Served locally on :8090, tested with Playwright/Chromium at 1440px and 390px. No sales numbers or customer data used.

## Summary
No blocking bugs. Cart links, variant IDs, prices, JSON, JSON-LD, internal links, gallery filters and viewer, and the mobile drawer all pass. The findings are accessibility and structure gaps, all low or medium.

## Findings

| # | Severity | Where | Finding | Suggested fix |
|---|----------|-------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | Medium (a11y) | 11 of 13 top-level pages (all except links.html) and all `prints/*.html` | No `<main>` landmark and no skip-to-content link. WCAG 2.1 SC 1.3.1, 2.4.1 (bypass blocks). Probe saw `nomain` on every page tested. | Wrap page content in `<main id="main">`, add a visually-hidden "Skip to content" link as the first focusable element. Print pages: change the template in `scripts/build_print_pages.py` and rebuild; never hand-edit. |
| 2 | Low (a11y) | links.html | No `<h1>` (count 0). | Make the page title an `<h1>`. |
| 3 | Low (SEO) | 404.html, thank-you.html | No meta description and no canonical. Both are probably meant to be `noindex` (thank-you has it); confirm 404 behaviour. | Add `noindex` to 404 if desired; descriptions are optional for these. |
| 4 | Low (consistency) | BUSINESS.md vs site | BUSINESS.md says Verdigris and Copper and Teal are ready to sell like any other print. Copper and Teal is in the gallery as an image (`images/portfolio/ocean/15-copper-and-teal`). Neither has a print page: the catalog has 65 prints, 66 print files including `index.html`, and no slug for either. | Manager/Logan: confirm they should be added to `prints/catalog.json` (Shopify variants must exist first; the team cannot create products). |
| 5 | Info | gallery.html | Gallery tiles link to `/contact?print=<Title>` (enquiry), while the lightbox "buy" links go to `/prints/<slug>`. Not broken, but the two paths differ. | Optional: point tiles at the print pages. Design decision, so backlog. |
| 6 | Info (environment) | all pages | Only console error on any page: `net::ERR_TUNNEL_CONNECTION_FAILED`. This is Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`) blocked by the sandbox proxy, not a site bug. No local 4xx responses and no JS exceptions. | None. Cloudflare will serve fonts in production. Note: Google Fonts is a render-blocking stylesheet; consider `preconnect` (already present) plus self-hosting as a later perf proposal. |
| 7 | Info | gallery.html | Image probe found one `<img>` with an empty `src` on gallery at both widths (likely the lightbox placeholder created by gallery.js before first open). No visual impact seen. | Low priority: set the lightbox `img` src only when opened, or leave `hidden`. Not reproduced as a visible bug. |

## Checks run and results
- Print pages: 65 of 65 loaded at 1440px and 390px with no local errors, no horizontal scroll, and no broken images.
- Cart links: all 1515 `https://sessjk-nj.myshopify.com/cart/<id>:1` links across `prints/*.html` use the right store, and every variant ID exists in `prints/catalog.json` (0 bad).
- Pickers (clicked every material tab and every frame swatch): 8 sample prints x 2 viewports, 210 visible buy buttons in total. Every href is a catalog variant for that print and the price shown matches the catalog. 0 failures. (Sample: blue-hour-ridge, obsidian-palace, shark-shadow, highland-hollow, into-the-unknown, aspen-and-cobalt.) Sample slugs `verdigris` and `copper-and-teal` do not exist (see finding 4).
- Top-level pages (13 plus prints/index): no horizontal scroll at 1440 or 390, no local 404s, `lang="en"` set, all images have alt, all form controls labelled.
- Mobile drawer: opens (aria-expanded true, full width, 7 links) and closes with Escape on index, about, events, contact and gallery. links, thank-you and 404 have no hamburger by design.
- Gallery: All shows 67 items; the Collections filters give Ocean 13, Galápagos 5, Salt and Stone 3, Mountains 16, Forests 12, Botanical 8, Urban 8, Portraits 4, Events 44. Lightbox opens (role=dialog, aria-modal), ArrowRight advances, Escape closes. Lightbox links point to `/prints/<slug>`.
- Contact: Formspree action set, required fields name/email/message, "How did you hear about me?" select present (7 options). Work-with-me and contact mailto: oxhollowmedia@gmail.com. Did not submit the form (would email externally).
- Internal references: 4308 href/src/srcset/data-preview checks across all HTML, 0 missing files.
- JSON: all `*.json` parse. JSON-LD blocks parse on every page, 0 failures.
- Sitemap: 76 URLs, all resolve to files, and every print page is listed. robots.txt and `_redirects` look sane. `target="_blank"` links all carry `noopener`.
