# Plan A: Event photography and brand films

## Who to target
1. **Event and marketing managers at Bay Area tech companies and startups** who run conferences, launches, demo days, offsites, holiday parties. They decide and book quickly.
2. **Event producers and planners** (agencies) who need a reliable photographer for client events. One relationship can mean repeat work.
3. **Venues** whose sales teams get asked "do you know a photographer?" (Moscone, Fort Mason, The Midway, Pier 27 and YBCA are already on /events as places you work).
4. **Nonprofits and conservation groups** for galas (they fit the film work too). Lower budget, strong brand fit.

## Channels ranked by expected return for a small budget
1. **Direct outreach to planners, agencies and venue sales teams ($0).** Highest return: one booking at $1,000-$2,000 (Half/Full day, per /events) outweighs any reasonable ad spend. Use the templates below and the weekly leads file.
2. **Google Business Profile as a service-area business, plus Search Console ($0).** "Event photographer near me" and local map searches. Needs verification time, so start now.
3. **Referral asks and preferred-vendor lists ($0).** Ask the people you have worked with for an introduction. Ask venues to list you as a vendor. TODO(Logan): which past clients are happy to be named or to give a one-line quote? (Semgrep quote is already a pending TODO on work-with-me.)
4. **Google Ads search, tightly targeted ($250+/month).** People searching "event photographer san francisco" already want to hire. Exact and phrase match only, SF Bay Area only, a small daily cap. Expect a handful of clicks per day at most; costs are unknown until you check Keyword Planner.
5. **LinkedIn (organic first, ads only at $750 and only as a test).** Your profile is already linked in the site's structured data. Post 2 event galleries a month and connect with event leads. Paid LinkedIn is the most expensive per click in the research and is the last thing to try.

Not recommended for this plan: Meta/Instagram ads for event leads. The audience is too broad for how few buyers exist. Use organic Reels as the portfolio.

2. **Nature, hiking, ocean-conservation and travel followers** on Instagram and Pinterest, including people who follow Eastern Sierra, Seattle, Colorado and Galápagos content.
3. **Interior designers, small offices and tech-company offices** for larger framed work. Slower, higher value per sale.
4. **People who already visited the shop:** retargeting and email.
