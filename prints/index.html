How it works

Made to order.

Open any print, choose a size, and check out securely through Stripe. Every print is made to order on archival matte paper and shipped to you, free anywhere in the US. You get a receipt by email right away and another email when your print ships.

If a photograph anywhere on the site caught your eye and it isn't listed here, ask. Most can be printed. oxhollowmedia@gmail.com

Questions

Before you order.

What paper are the prints made on?
Archival matte fine-art paper, 200gsm. It has no glare and holds fine detail in shadows and skies.
What sizes and prices are available?
Four sizes: 8×12″ for $55, 12×18″ for $100, 16×24″ for $175, and 24×36″ for $320. Shipping is included.
Where do you ship, and how long does it take?
Anywhere in the US, free. Allow a few days for printing plus transit. I'll email you when it ships.
What if my print arrives damaged or wrong?
Email a photo within 14 days and I'll send a free replacement. See shipping & returns.
Can I order a photograph that isn't listed here?
Yes. Almost anything in the gallery can be printed. Send me the title.
Do you offer framing?
Prints ship unframed. If you'd like help with framing, get in touch.
Do you sell limited editions?
Signed, numbered editions are available on select frames. Ask and I'll tell you what's still open.
Licensing instead of a print?
High-resolution files can be licensed for personal or commercial use. Get in touch with what you have in mind.
