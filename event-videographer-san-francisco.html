FAQ

Answers, up front.

What does an event recap video include?
A 60 to 120 second highlight film cut to music, plus vertical cut-downs for Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok. Interviews, speaker capture, and a longer edit can be added.
Can one person cover both photo and video?
Yes, for smaller events. For conferences and galas I recommend a second shooter so the photo gallery and the film both get full attention. Either way you have one point of contact.
How long does an event video take to deliver?
Social cut-downs within a week and the finished recap film within about two weeks, depending on length and revisions.
How much does event videography cost in San Francisco?
Event video is quoted per event based on hours, crew, and deliverables. Combined photo and film coverage starts at $6,500. Send me your run-of-show for an exact quote.
