# Ox Hollow second brain: social growth

A living knowledge base. Everything the team and Logan learn about growing an audience goes here, in small files that get updated, not replaced. Research lives in `../knowledge/`; this folder is what we *believe and do* because of it.

**The loop:** idea → `inbox.md` → experiment in `experiments/` → post logged in `post-log.md` → result → rule in `learnings.md` → update `strategy.md`. The Monday team run walks this loop every week (see "Weekly review" below).

## Map

| File | What it holds | Who updates it |
|---|---|---|
| [identity.md](identity.md) | Who Logan is on social, the two accounts, voice, what's off-limits | Logan decides; team records |
| [audience.md](audience.md) | Who we're for, what they want, where they are | Team, from research and comments |
| [strategy.md](strategy.md) | The current plan: positioning, pillars, cadence, 90-day goal | Manager, after each review |

| [formats/](formats/) | One card per content format: structure, hook, example, results so far | Design specialist + marketing lead |

| [post-log.md](post-log.md) | Every post on every platform with 7-day and 30-day numbers | Manager (from Metricool) |
| [learnings.md](learnings.md) | Distilled rules we now follow, with confidence and evidence | Manager, Logan can edit |
| [swipe-file.md](swipe-file.md) | Hooks, captions and posts we admired, with why | Anyone |
| [inbox.md](inbox.md) | Raw ideas, notes from Logan, things to look into | Logan dumps; manager triages Monday |
| [weekly-review.md](weekly-review.md) | The template the manager fills each Monday | Manager |

## Weekly review (Monday team run)
1. Pull last week's numbers from Metricool into `post-log.md`.
2. Close any experiment whose window ended: write the verdict, move the rule into `learnings.md`.
3. Triage `inbox.md`: turn ideas into experiments or drop them with a one-line reason.
4. Update `strategy.md` only if a learning changed it; note the change at the top with the date.
5. Plan next week's posts from `formats/` and the strategy rotation; put drafts in Dropbox Instagram Drafts.
6. Report to Logan: what won, what we changed, what needs him.

## Rules for writing here
- Short. One idea per paragraph. Dates on anything that can go stale.
- Evidence beats opinion: link the post, the thread, the number.
- Never invent facts about Logan's trips, clients or results. Leave `TODO(Logan)` and ask.
- Logan's own notes are the most valuable thing in here. Keep them verbatim in `inbox.md` and quote them.
