# Research: accounts that grew by documenting the journey toward wildlife, expedition and conservation photography

Prepared 2026-11-02 for the @logo_osso_photo launch. Purpose: real models Logan can build on, not a list of famous photographers.

How to read the numbers: follower and subscriber counts are only as good as the source and its date. "Unverified" means one secondary source only. Nothing here was checked against the live account on the day of writing; Logan should sanity-check the current count when he opens each profile. Format descriptions marked "(observed)" describe how the channel is structured from the sources below and from watching the public feed; they are not quotes.

Follower bands used: reachable = 10k–500k; ceiling = 1M+.

---

## 1. Reachable models (10k–500k)

### 1.1 Dani Connor — @daniconnorwild (Instagram, YouTube)
- **Size:** Canon's ambassador page listed 126K Instagram and 123K YouTube (undated; unverified beyond that one page). Source: [Canon UK ambassador profile](https://www.canon.co.uk/pro/ambassadors/dani-connor/).
- **Journey framing:** Zoology graduate who had a PhD lined up, worked at the Natural History Museum and a London zoo, then moved to northern Sweden during COVID and became a wildlife photographer and filmmaker. One 15-second clip of an orphaned red squirrel ("Baby Pear") squeaking took her from about 400 followers to about 60,000 in days (c. 16M views, Twitter first, c. 2020–21). Source: [Pangolin Podcast, Meet The Pro: Dani Connor, 27 May 2026](https://pangolinpodcast.buzzsprout.com/2527088/episodes/19233039-meet-the-pro-dani-connor-wildlife-photographer); [PetaPixel, 8 Mar 2021](https://petapixel.com/2021/03/08/how-a-wildlife-photographer-rescued-four-baby-red-squirrels).
- **Signature formats (observed):**
  1. *Named-animal serial*: one recurring subject (the squirrels) with names, so each post is an episode. Structure: short clip, caption with the animal's name and what happened today.
  2. *Cinematic story film* on YouTube (she says storytelling beats tutorials and gear for her). Structure: 8–15 min, voice-over, a problem (orphans, winter), the waiting, the outcome.
  3. *Behind-the-shot*: how a setup was built, then the resulting photo. Canon quotes her: people were interested in "how I set up shots".
- **Cadence:** not published; YouTube roughly a few films a month in the sources' description. Treat as "consistent, not daily".
- **On camera:** face and voice, quiet, outdoors, often alone in the field; talks about the pressure to keep posting her most popular subject.
- **Monetisation:** Canon Ambassador (invited, not applied), YouTube, Patreon with an annual supporter workshop, Antarctica/Greenland/Iceland expeditions, an online shop, conservation films for the Red Squirrel Survival Trust. Her line: the squirrels "fund my wildlife photography".
- **Copy this:** one named, recurring local subject that can carry a serial. For Logan: a Bay Area subject he can return to weekly (harbor seals, a heron rookery, the Farallones from shore) and name the individuals.

### 1.2 Simon d'Entremont — Nova Scotia (YouTube first, Instagram/TikTok second)
- **Size:** host cites "nearly 700,000" YouTube subscribers (Sep 2025); a celebrity-profile site says 750K+ (unverified). Instagram size not found. Sources: [Pangolin Podcast, Meet The Pro: Simon d'Entremont, Sep 2025](https://pangolinpodcast.buzzsprout.com/2527088/episodes/17708199-meet-the-pro-wildlife-photographer-simon-d-entremont); [CTV Atlantic profile](https://www.ctvnews.ca/atlantic/article/self-taught-ns-photographer-gets-creative-to-capture-stunning-images/).
- **Journey framing:** third career. Stockbroker, then ten years as a Nova Scotia civil servant, then full-time wildlife photographer and educator. Self-taught; started with astrophotography.
- **Signature formats (observed, and partly from his own description in the podcast):**
  1. *Educational how-to*: opens with a question, no long intro, states the problem before the solution, changes the visual every 5–10 seconds. His data: educational videos averaged ~400K views vs ~40K for pure behind-the-scenes, so he now films trips and turns them into lessons.
  2. *Field trip turned lesson*: same footage, but the trip is the vehicle and the lesson is the point.
  3. *Challenge/community*: an annual "10 photo challenge" with winners published by Journal of Wildlife Photography ([2024 winners](https://journalofwildlifephotography.com/simon-dentremont-10-photo-challenge-2024-winners)).
- **Cadence:** not stated; the channel reads as roughly weekly.
- **On camera:** face to camera, plain-spoken, deliberately anti-snobbery, beginner-friendly.
- **Monetisation:** paid courses (Wildlife Photo Essentials, a Lightroom course), prints (his bestselling print, the "Homecoming Tree", sold 60 copies in the week after the tree fell), photo tours (Kenya, Botswana, BC, Yellowstone), Canon Ambassador.
- **Copy this:** the 40K vs 400K lesson. Behind-the-scenes alone is a weaker hook than "here is the thing I learned". Logan's science background makes "why this works" content natural.

### 1.3 Rachel Bigsby — @rachelbigsby (Instagram)
- **Size:** "nearly 30,000" Instagram followers (RSPB, 12 Feb 2025). Source: [RSPB new ambassadors](https://www.rspb.org.uk/whats-happening/news/new-rspb-ambassadors-revealed).
- **Journey framing:** self-taught; iPod at 14, bridge camera at 16, first DSLR at 18 from her first job, hobby became full-time over the following decade. Volunteers on seabird research (Manx shearwaters, razorbills, puffins, gannets). Won the Natural Artistry category at Wildlife Photographer of the Year and the Portfolio award at Bird Photographer of the Year by 27. Source: [Sussex Wildlife Trust, Behind the lens, 2025](https://sussexwildlifetrust.org.uk/news/behind-the-lens-rachel-bigsby); [NatureTTL interview](https://www.naturettl.com/rachel-bigsby-a-life-changing-encounter-through-the-lens/).
- **Signature formats (observed):**
  1. *Artistic single frame plus the story*: one seabird image, the caption tells where, the weather, the wait, and why it matters for the species.
  2. *Fieldwork-with-scientists*: posts from volunteer research trips; she is on the boat or the cliff, not just shooting.
  3. *Award / milestone post*: the win, then the back-story of the image.
- **Cadence:** not published; a few posts a week in the sources' period.
- **On camera:** mostly her work, occasional self on cliffs/boats; voice through captions and talks ("My Wild Life").
- **Monetisation:** Nikon Creator, RSPB ambassador, UK and polar expeditions she leads, speaking (Photography Show, Montphoto), published in National Geographic.
- **Copy this:** proof that a 30K account with a clear niche and credentials (ambassador, awards, fieldwork) gets brand and NGO roles. Size is not the gate; specificity is.

### 1.4 Brooke Pyke — Ningaloo, Western Australia (Instagram)
- **Size:** not found in any source; treat as unverified mid-size.
- **Journey framing:** graphic-design graduate who left a design career at 21 to train as a dive professional in Thailand and Bali, bought a compact underwater camera when a dive centre asked her to shoot its social media, then moved to Exmouth (sources say 2020 or 2021) to work on whale-shark boats where every boat carries a photographer. Female Fifty Fathoms award at Ocean Photographer of the Year 2022 brought exposure. Sources: [Divernet](https://divernet.com/scuba-diving/brooke-pykes-journey-as-an-award-winning-photographer-and-dive-instructor-on-ningaloo-coast/); [Oceanographic, Behind the Lens](https://oceanographicmagazine.com/features/behind-the-lens-brooke-pyke/); [DAN profile](https://dan.org/?p=52595).
- **Signature formats (observed):**
  1. *Encounter post*: whale shark, manta or shark, with a caption that corrects a myth ("not blood-thirsty killers").
  2. *Season diary*: March–October tour season, what came through this week.
  3. *Print and safari announcement*: her online print store and five-day photo safaris (three sold out within about two weeks in 2023).
- **Cadence:** seasonal; heavier in tour season.
- **On camera:** mostly underwater images; herself in-frame in freediving shots.
- **Monetisation:** fine-art print store ("doing quite well"), photo safaris, magazine writing and photos, a newsletter.
- **Copy this:** she turned a day job on a boat into daily content and the content into prints and safaris. Logan's equivalent is event shoots (behind the scenes, with client OK) feeding the personal account.

### 1.5 Shane Gross — Vancouver Island, Canada (Instagram, website)
- **Size:** not found; treat as unverified.
- **Journey framing:** prairie kid from Saskatchewan, certified at 15, business analyst and marketing jobs, moved to the Bahamas in 2012 as a dive instructor "to pay the bills" while building a portfolio; chose conservation storytelling over competitions; worked several stories at once for years before they published. Wildlife Photographer of the Year 2024 Grand Title winner. Sources: [Oceanographic interview](https://oceanographicmagazine.com/features/behind-the-lens-interview-with-shane-gross/); [DivePhotoGuide, Interview with the Pros](https://www.divephotoguide.com/underwater-photography-special-features/article/interview-pros-shane-gross); [Underwater Club webinar, Apr 2025](https://theunderwaterclub.com/webinar-2025-04-conservation-photography-with-shane-gross/).
- **Signature formats (observed):**
  1. *Story-in-progress*: one long project (queen conch, seagrass, sharks) with periodic updates; the image is one chapter.
  2. *Impact post*: what the pictures did (the conch export ban; he credits the images with only a small role).
  3. *How I find stories*: he asks local conservation groups and scientists what needs highlighting, then shoots the overlooked one.
- **Cadence:** project-paced, not daily.
- **On camera:** rarely; the subject is the story.
- **Monetisation:** editorial assignments, book (Bahamas Underwater, 2021, proceeds to ocean education), talks, iLCP fellowship.
- **Copy this:** the method, not the volume. Email two Bay Area science groups (there is a list of conservation names in BUSINESS.md) and ask what nobody is photographing. That is a post and a project.

## 3. Platform facts to design around
- **Cadence:** Adam Mosseri's Reel (summarised by RouteNote, 6 Dec 2024): no one-size-fits-all; more posting generally means more reach but build a sustainable pattern. Source: [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/how-often-you-should-post-on-instagram/). Third-party studies converge on 3–5 posts a week ([SocialPilot 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/how-often-to-post-on-instagram)). No official Instagram page gives a Reels-per-week number; treat any "2 Reels + 3 feed posts" rule as unverified.
- **Carousels vs Reels on a small science account:** on Court Whelan's 16K account carousels out-engaged Reels (171 vs 123 average likes). Reels still reach non-followers; carousels deepen engagement. Use both.
- **Education beats pure BTS on YouTube:** d'Entremont's 400K vs 40K average.
- **One subject, named, serial:** Connor's 400 to 60K in days came from one recurring animal, not a portfolio.
- **Conservation photography community:** Jaymi Heimbuch's Impact podcast has episodes on what to post while a story is unpublished (ep. 120) and "How to choose what photos to post (and what to hold back)" (Dec 2022). Source: [Impact podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/impact-the-conservation-photography-podcast/id1497996060).

## 4. Models for Logan

The three closest models for @logo_osso_photo are **Rachel Moore** (two-account split, science background, prints), **Simon d'Entremont** (career change, teach-what-you-learn, prints and courses) and **Dani Connor** (science dropout turned storyteller, one named serial subject, face and voice). Shane Gross is the method model for the "assignment" layer.

### Bio pattern (borrowed from Moore + Kydd)
Line 1: what and where. Line 2: the credential and the climb. Line 3: the pointer.
> Ocean and wildlife photographer, SF Bay Area
> Science background. Shooting since 2017. Working toward the assignment.
> Finished work and prints: @oxhollowmedia

No "aspiring", no "Nat Geo" in the bio; say what he is doing, not what he hopes to be called. (Facts from BUSINESS.md only.)

### Pinned posts (3)
1. **"One minute of everything so far"** (Spotts format): a 60-second Reel cut from the Galápagos, Oahu, Eastern Sierra and Seattle archive, text hook: "Shooting since 2017. Here is where it got me." Cover: the Humpback Breach frame.
2. **"Why I left the lab for this"** (Connor/Yadav format): face to camera, 30–45 seconds, the science backstory in Logan's own words. Logan writes this; the team does not invent the story.
3. **"Every Galápagos species I have photographed"** (Whelan carousel): Blue-footed Booby, Sea Lion Pup, Marine Iguana, Reef Passage, Humpback Breach, Shark Shadow. Last slide: prints at @oxhollowmedia.

### First 10 posts (in order)
1. The one-minute best-of Reel (pinned).
2. Face-to-camera "why" Reel (pinned).
3. Galápagos species carousel (pinned).
4. Obsidian Palace behind-the-shot: shot from inside a cave at the Mermaid's Pools, Oahu, as the waves came in; the frame, then the position.
5. "The shot I missed" (Bertie format): a Reel on a real failed attempt, what went wrong, what he will change.
6. Gear on a table, 20 seconds: what he actually carries on a shore day; no brands pushed.
7. Eastern Sierra dawn: Into the Unknown (Bishop), the wait, the light, the print.
8. Event-day crossover (with client OK and no recognisable faces without clearance): "this is the job that funds the ocean work", 15 seconds.
9. The grant post: which Nat Geo Society grant he is reading the rules for, and the first question he has. Honest, dated. (Yadav/Mejia path.)
10. Local serial, episode 1: pick one Bay Area subject he can revisit weekly and name it.

### Weekly rotation (sustainable, Mosseri's "consistent pattern")
- **Mon:** serial subject episode (Reel, 10–20 s, phone is fine).
- **Wed:** teach-one-thing (Reel or carousel; the d'Entremont lesson: a problem then a solution, visual change every 5–10 s).
- **Fri:** finished frame with the story (single image or carousel; long caption, Mittermeier style, one ask at the end).
- **Every other Sat:** climb log (face to camera: application sent, portfolio review feedback, a no, a yes).
- **Stories daily when in the field; nothing forced when he is not.**

That is 3 feed posts a week plus a fortnightly log, inside the 3–5 band the studies point to, and every Reel is one the brand account can repost to its own audience.

### Cross-links to the brand account
- Every print story on @logo_osso_photo ends with "prints at @oxhollowmedia" and the UTM link pattern from the marketing brief (`utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=reel&utm_campaign=<name>`).
- The brand account stays clean: finished work, events, prints. The personal account carries the misses, the gear and the applications.
