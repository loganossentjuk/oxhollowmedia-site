# Learnings: rules we follow now

Format: **Rule.** Confidence (low/med/high). Evidence. Date.

- **Text on a Reel goes on a solid cream band in the top third; never thin serif over a busy photo.** High. Logan rejected the first TikTok as unreadable (2026-10-28); the cream-band version was accepted. 2026-10-28.
- **Silent videos don't ship.** High. Logan: "It can't be silent." TikTok web uploader only has the royalty-free library; trending sounds must be added in the app. 2026-10-28.
- **A slideshow of finished prints with facts is not enough for the personal account.** High. Logan rejected the Galápagos "lizard sneezes salt" Reel: "show off the work I've done… striving Nat Geo photographer… learning and growing." 2026-11-02.
- **Pinterest pins in the framed-black-on-cream style are approved as a standing format.** High. Logan: "Those look amazing." 2026-10-28.
- **Keep the brand account clean; put the journey on the personal account.** High. Logan's decision, 2026-11-02.
- **One account, one platform, 90 days: @oxhollowmedia on Instagram, with Logan on voice/camera; @logo_osso_photo parked.** High. Logan's decision after the Reddit and models research, 2026-11-02.
- **Instagram is proof, not the funnel. Events pay; prints come from search; say so in every report.** High. Reddit threads + Logan agreed, 2026-11-02.

- **Team-built Reels: 16:9, no text, no cards.** Logan adds text and sound in the app. (Logan, 2026-11-02; rule, high confidence.)
- **Every post ends with one ask**, rotated: prints / events / follow / room. (Logan approved 2026-11-02.)
- **Brand voice is humble and nature-first.** Taglines/hooks that lead with Logan's achievement read as arrogant to him. Lead with the place, the animal, the unseen; Logan is the guide. (Logan, 2026-11-04; rule, high confidence.)
