About

Behind the lens.

I am a professional photographer, videographer, science storyteller, and explorer. I seek to tell stories of how people and the environment come together, forming new connections and inspirations along the way.

Every frame begins the same way — quietly, on foot, waiting for the light to reveal something worth remembering. Based in the San Francisco Bay Area and shaped by the coastlines and high country of the West, the work moves between ocean, mountains, and the faces in between.

The name

Where Ox Hollow
comes from.

Ox Hollow is an homage to where I came from and where I will go. My name is Logan Ossentjuk — etymologically, Logan means “little hollow,” and Ossentjuk means “oxen yoke.” Combined to create new meaning, Ox Hollow was born.
