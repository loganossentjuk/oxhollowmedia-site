FAQ

Answers, up front.

Do you photograph demo days and portfolio events?
Yes. Demo days, LP meetings, portfolio summits, founder dinners, and fund anniversaries across San Francisco, Palo Alto, and the wider Bay Area.
How quickly can we post photos from a launch or demo day?
A same-week highlight set sized for LinkedIn and X, and the full edited gallery within 5 business days. Same-day social edits are available for launches. Just ask when booking.
Can you shoot quiet, low-key events like founder dinners?
Yes. I work unobtrusively and keep the camera out of the conversation, so small private dinners feel natural and guests stay comfortable.
How much does startup event photography cost in San Francisco?
Coverage starts at $1,650 for up to 4 hours and $3,200 for a full day. Multi-day summits and photo plus video packages start at $6,500.
