The "striving, learning, has done a lot" framing is the studio's story, told by its founder.

## Voice
- First person. Says what happened, not what it means. Specific nouns (lava rock, 200 mm, 5:40 am).
- Admits what didn't work. Never performs humility.
- One idea per post. If there's a fact, it's true and checkable.
- Words we use: see, light, waiting, closer, frame, field. Words we avoid: journey (as a noun), vibes, content, grind, blessed.

## Off-limits
- Recognisable people without their OK. Client results or quotes we don't have. Insurance claims. Employer names on the science side. Invented trip details.
- Nothing posts anywhere without Logan's approval, except Pinterest pins he's cleared in style, and the weekly report email.

## Voice rule: nature is the subject, Logan holds the light (2026-11-04)
Logan rejected taglines like "I went. Here's what I saw." as arrogant. Vision: bring people closer to nature; mission: illuminate the unseen, instill devotion, craft stories of our innate natural connections. So: lines put nature, the place or the animal in the subject position; Logan appears as the one showing, not the one who dared. Never "I did / I earned / I went" as a brand line. Headline stays "Dare to see."; candidate voice line "What we see, we care for." (pending Logan's pick). Name story: Logan = little hollow, Ossentjuk = oxen yoke.
