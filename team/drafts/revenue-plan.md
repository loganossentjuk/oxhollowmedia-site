**Scale check:** one Session booking ($1,000) earns about as much as 11 framed 12x18 orders do in contribution (11 × $88). One content-partner month ($2,500) equals about 28. Events and retainers lead the plan, as Logan decided.

## 2. Targets: 90 days and 12 months (bottom-up, gross revenue)

### Assumptions
- **Average event booking value:**
  - $1,200 conservative: mostly Sessions, with occasional overtime or rush
  - $1,500 base: a Session/Day mix plus some add-ons
  - $1,800 stretch: more Days, second shooters and overtime
- **Enquiry → booking rate.** There's no reliable published benchmark.
  - One photography coach plans on 50%, but that's a rule of thumb **(unverified)** [R5].
  - A HoneyBook survey of 425 photographers found 30.4% lose bookings by replying too slowly [R6].
  - A widely repeated claim says replies within 1 hour convert 3.2x better **(unverified, original report not found)** [R7].
  - **Plan on 33% until Logan's own numbers exist.** That means about 3 qualified enquiries per booking.
- **Print store conversion.** Littledata's Shopify median is 1.4%: bottom 20% at 0.5%, top 20% at 2.7%, top 10% at 3.5%. That's 426 stores, Jul 6 – Oct 3, 2026 [R1].
  - A brand-new store with no reviews should plan at **0.5–1.4%**.
  - Home decor and art convert lower than the average: about 1.2% (Statista, cited by MoEngage) **(unverified)** [R2].
- **Prints AOV:** $130 conservative, $155 base, $165 stretch. Some orders will be multi-item or 16x24.
- **Retainer:** one content-partner client is $2,500/mo.
- **Window:** 90 days = Oct 19, 2026 – Jan 17, 2027, which covers holiday-party season and January kickoffs. 12 months = Oct 2026 – Sep 2027.

### 90-day targets

| Scenario | Events | Retainer | Prints | **Total** | What it takes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 2 × $1,200 = $2,400 | none | 3 × $130 = $390 | **≈ $2,800** | ~6 qualified enquiries; ~600 store sessions at 0.5% |
| Base | 4 × $1,500 = $6,000 | none | 8 × $155 = $1,240 | **≈ $7,200** | ~12 enquiries (about 1 a week); ~570 sessions at 1.4% |
| Stretch | 6 × $1,800 = $10,800 | 1 client starting January, 1 month = $2,500 | 15 × $165 = $2,475 | **≈ $15,800** | ~18 enquiries plus a retainer close; ~550–1,100 sessions at 1.4–2.7% |

### 12-month targets

| Scenario | Events | Retainers | Films | Prints | **Total** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 10 × $1,200 = $12,000 | none | none | 20 × $130 = $2,600 | **≈ $14,600** |
| Base | 18 × $1,500 = $27,000 | 1 client × 6 mo = $15,000 | none | 40 × $155 = $6,200 | **≈ $48,200** |
| Stretch | 30 × $1,800 = $54,000 | 1 × 10 mo + 1 × 6 mo = $40,000 | 1 × $8,000 (low end of the stated $8k–20k) | 80 × $165 = $13,200 | **≈ $115,200** |

- These are gross revenue. Events and retainers carry Logan's time plus the unknown costs in §1. Prints net about 57% of price.
- **Base needs about 1.5 bookings a month and one retainer.** At 33%, that's about 4–5 qualified enquiries a month. The question that decides the scenario is how many enquiries the site and outreach produce now: that's a question for Logan, and GA4 `generate_lead` will answer it from here on.

## 4. Pricing and offer proposals (Logan approves; nothing has been changed)

1. **Holiday-party Session.**
   - The Session at its current price, named and positioned for December parties.
   - Same-week highlight set included. Today that's a Day-only feature, so offering it here is a new commitment for Logan to decide on.
   - The rush add-on ($200) is offered for next-day selects.
2. **Retainer starter term.**
   - A 3-month content-partner commitment at the listed $2,500/mo, so a first-time buyer has a defined trial.
   - Or a quarterly invoice, but only if it fits the no-deposit rule (Logan's call).
3. **Event recap-cut add-on.**
   - Films start at $6,500, which leaves a gap for event clients who want a short video.
   - A fixed-price 60–90 s recap cut, priced by Logan, is a natural Day/Production upsell.
   - **TODO(Logan):** would you offer it, and at what price?
4. **Price review rule, not a change.**
   - Market quotes cluster above $250/h **(unverified)** [R3][R4].
   - After 8–10 quotes, if more than 70% book, treat it as a sign the Session is underpriced and review it then.
   - If under 25% book, look at reply speed and fit before price.
5. **Prints: no price change** (growth-shop-input §2). Use non-price offers instead:
   - gift-deadline banner
   - gift cards at $115 / $155 / $205
   - Embers set cart link
   - gift guide by price point
